<objective>
Produce a cited audit of the legacy `Locations` and `Resources` controllers and their `Location`/`Resource` models — the bookable reference entities every Booking attaches to. Confirm the exact Location display-metadata field set (CSS class/colour, building, layout) and the deletion-in-use policy, both flagged as unconfirmed in the FRD.

Purpose: Give Phase 4 (Reference Data, Extensibility & Configuration) a confirmed legacy baseline for Location/Resource CRUD instead of FRD's interim assumptions.
Output: `.planning/phases/01-legacy-functional-audit/findings/02-reference-data.md`
</objective>

</feature_dependencies>
